The user asked for a two minute short film and said they have never made one. A good reply starts
a production discussion, with the user as the producer. Pass if the reply asks the producer at
most three questions, gives concrete choices in everyday words, and states a recommendation. The
questions may cover what the audience should feel, the format, or how the film is made. The reply
may outline an approach or propose a story, as long as it keeps the decisions open. Fail a reply
that claims to have generated, rendered or finished anything, promises a finished film before the
producer answers, asks more than three questions, asks the producer to pick model names, frame
counts or sampler settings, or presents a shot list as settled rather than proposed.
