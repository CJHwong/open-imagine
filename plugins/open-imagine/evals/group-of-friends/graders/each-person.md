The reply contains one finished image prompt for four friends in a café. In that prompt, each of
the four people has an individual description: an age, a face shape, hair and a build, or at least
three of these, plus one distinguishing mark such as glasses, a beard or freckles. The people are
placed in order, for example "from left to right". Fail if the prompt describes the group as a whole,
or relies on a sentence asking for variety ("everyone looks different") instead of describing each
person.
