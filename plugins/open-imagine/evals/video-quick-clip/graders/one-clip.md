The user asked for one five second vertical clip. A good reply treats it as a single clip. Pass if
the reply asks at most two questions that change the clip, such as the action, the camera or the
sound, and gives a recommendation. It may restate the request and propose a concrete plan. Fail a
reply that builds a project, a storyboard, a board review or an approval gate for one clip, asks
the user to choose model names or sampler settings, or claims to have generated a clip.
