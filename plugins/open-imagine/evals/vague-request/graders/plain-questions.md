The user asked for "a nice picture of my cat" and said they know nothing about photography. A good
reply plans to make the picture, and asks before it makes anything. Pass only if the reply asks what
the picture is for (such as a phone wallpaper, a post, or a print) and offers numbered or bulleted
picks in everyday words. It asks three questions or fewer, and none about camera settings such as
focal length, aperture or ISO. Fail a reply that only gives the user tips for taking the photo
themselves, or presents a finished prompt as final without asking.
