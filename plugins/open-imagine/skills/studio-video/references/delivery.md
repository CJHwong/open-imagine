# Delivery

Give the producer files they can play, and say what you checked.

- **Names.** Use a descriptive name: subject, action and version. Keep the request and provenance beside each final, so the work can be repeated or revised.
- **Files.** Do not re-encode the engine's MP4 without a reason. When you make a lighter copy, keep the original.
- **Audio.** Report integrated loudness and true peak. The true peak limit is -1 dBTP, as `edit_check.py` enforces. State the measured numbers rather than "loud enough".
- **Text.** Captions and subtitles are a separate layer. The video model may draw words into the picture. Check the frames before you promise a clean plate.
- **Review page.** Open it and look before you hand it over. Remove local paths and failure text. Publishing is a separate action.
- **Report.** Say what you watched, what you measured in the clip, and what remains unassessed. A passing script, a clean frame sheet or a finished operation is not a quality result.

A storyboard, an animatic and a finished film are different deliverables. Name the one you made.
